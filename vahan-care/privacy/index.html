Privacy Policy — Vahan Care

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Lumiten Systems ("we") publishes the Vahan Care mobile app ("the app"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short: Vahan Care stores everything you enter only on your device. We do not operate servers for the app, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

Information you enter

  • Vehicle details: registration number, nickname, make, model, year, fuel type, registration date, and any notes you add (for example chassis number or tyre size).
  • Document details: insurance, PUC, RC and road-tax dates and notes; other documents such as FASTag, warranty, loan / EMI, purchase invoice or roadside assistance, including any reference numbers you enter; and optional photos of documents.
  • Service and fuel logs, including costs, garage names, odometer readings and optional receipt photos.

This information is saved in a local database and the app's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage; the originals in your gallery are untouched. The app needs no account or sign-in and makes no network requests with your data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to remind you before documents expire and services fall due. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Camera and photos — only when you choose to attach a photo of a document or receipt.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app (deleting a vehicle removes all its records and photos) or uninstall the app, which removes all app data. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover lost data. Backups made by your phone's operating system (for example Google backup) are governed by your provider's policy.

Children

The app is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 18.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. Because all data is held on your device, you can do this directly in the app. For any questions or grievances, contact our Grievance Officer at lumitensystems@gmail.com.

Not affiliated with any government body

Vahan Care is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Parivahan, the VAHAN system or any insurer.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
